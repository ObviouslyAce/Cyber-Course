Scams and Fraud

Scams and fraud can affect anyone. Learning how to identify and avoid them is key to protecting yourself online.

Malware

Malware (short for malicious software) is any software designed to harm, exploit, or otherwise compromise a device, network, or user. Malware can steal personal data, disrupt system operations, or give attackers unauthorized access. It often spreads through infected files, links, or software downloads.

  • Malware can disguise itself as legitimate software, tricking users into downloading and running it unknowingly.
  • Businesses are common targets, with malware used to steal customer data or hold systems hostage for ransom.
  • Keeping software updated helps protect against malware by patching security vulnerabilities attackers might exploit.

Social Engineering

Social engineering is a manipulation tactic where attackers trick individuals into revealing confidential information or performing actions that compromise security. Instead of hacking systems directly, these attackers exploit human psychology. Common techniques include impersonating authority figures, creating fake emergencies, or using persuasive language to bypass skepticism.

  • Scammers may pose as delivery services or banks, asking you to “confirm” personal details through fake forms.
  • Phone calls and text messages can be just as dangerous as emails—they may pressure you to act quickly or threaten account suspension.
  • Even small talk or casual questions can be used to gather bits of information for future attacks.

Common Scam Examples

  • "You've won a prize!" emails asking for bank info.
  • Tech support scams claiming your device is infected.
  • Romance scams building trust over time then asking for money.
