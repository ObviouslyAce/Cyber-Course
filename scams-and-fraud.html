Scams and Fraud

Scams and fraud can affect anyone. Learning how to identify and avoid them is key to protecting yourself online.

Phishing

Phishing is when scammers impersonate legitimate institutions to trick you into giving away sensitive data like passwords or credit card numbers.

  • Look out for suspicious emails, especially those asking for personal info.
  • Hover over links to check if the destination matches the sender.
  • Don’t download attachments from unknown sources.

Social Engineering

Social engineering involves manipulating people into revealing confidential information.

  • Scammers may impersonate co-workers or IT personnel.
  • They use urgency, fear, or curiosity to get you to act.
  • Always verify identities before sharing information.

Common Scam Examples

  • "You've won a prize!" emails asking for bank info.
  • Tech support scams claiming your device is infected.
  • Romance scams building trust over time then asking for money.
